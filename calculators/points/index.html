License Points & Suspension Risk Calculator

By Editorial Team, TicketCostGuide · Last reviewed 2026-10-18

Most states suspend a license at roughly 12 points within 12–24 months (thresholds vary: e.g., 15/24mo in Georgia, 12/12mo in New York's system, 4 in 12mo under Nevada-style demerit rules). Point values are typical (2–6 per moving violation); your state page lists exact values. Not legal advice.

How point systems work

Most states assign points per conviction, keep them on a rolling 12–36 month window, and trigger escalating consequences — warning letters, mandatory courses, then suspension — at set thresholds. A handful of states (e.g., Kansas, Louisiana, Minnesota, Mississippi, Oregon, Rhode Island, Washington, Wyoming, Hawaii) use no point system and instead suspend for repeat convictions directly.
