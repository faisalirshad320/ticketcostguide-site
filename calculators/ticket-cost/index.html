Speeding Ticket Cost Calculator

By Editorial Team, TicketCostGuide · Last reviewed 2026-10-15

Enter your state and how far over the limit you were cited, and this tool estimates your total out-of-pocket cost — base fine plus typical surcharges and court costs. Exact amounts are set by the court on your citation; use your state's page (linked in the result) for the sourced fine table.

Estimate combines typical statutory fine brackets with average mandatory surcharges and court costs. School/work-zone multipliers reflect common doubling rules. Not legal advice; your citation controls.

How speeding ticket totals are built

Your final bill is rarely just the statutory fine. Most states add court costs, a state assessment or surcharge, and sometimes county add-ons — items that frequently exceed the base fine itself. School-zone and work-zone citations commonly double the fine portion. The state pages on this site itemize each component with its legal source.

This page is general information, not legal advice. Fines, surcharges and court costs vary by county and court — always verify the amount on your citation or with the court listed on it. See our full disclaimer and editorial policy.
