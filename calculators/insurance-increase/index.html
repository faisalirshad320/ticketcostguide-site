Insurance Increase Calculator

By Editorial Team, TicketCostGuide · Last reviewed 2026-10-16

One speeding ticket raises US car insurance premiums by about 20–25% on average (roughly $300–$550/yr on a typical policy), while a DUI or reckless driving can raise them 60–110%. Enter your premium and violation to estimate your increase over the 3–5 years insurers look back.

Based on published market averages; actual increases depend on insurer, state rules and your record. Some states restrict surcharges for first minor offenses.

Why the insurance hit outlasts the fine

Insurers re-rate your policy at renewal after a conviction appears on your motor vehicle record, and most look back 3–5 years. That is why a $180 ticket can quietly cost $1,000+ — and why dismissal options like defensive driving courses or deferred adjudication (covered on each state's "fight your ticket" page) are often worth more than the fine reduction itself.
