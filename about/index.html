About TicketCostGuide

By Editorial Team, TicketCostGuide · Last reviewed 2026-10-15

TicketCostGuide is an independent reference site that documents what traffic tickets and court fines actually cost in the United States. We publish the current base fine ranges set by state statute, the surcharges and court costs added on top, license point values, record-retention periods, insurance impact, and the legal options for reducing or dismissing a citation — for all 50 states and the most common violation types.

Why we built it

Fine information is scattered across statutes, DMV handbooks and individual court fee schedules, and much of what ranks in search is outdated or written to sell legal services. TicketCostGuide exists to give drivers one neutral place with the current numbers and a citation for every figure.

How the site is funded

The site is free to use and supported by advertising. Advertisers never influence the figures we publish. See our editorial policy for how data is sourced and reviewed.

This page is general information, not legal advice. Fines, surcharges and court costs vary by county and court — always verify the amount on your citation or with the court listed on it. See our full disclaimer and editorial policy.
