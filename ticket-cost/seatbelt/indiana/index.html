How much is a seatbelt ticket in Indiana?

Nationally, adult seatbelt fines run $10–$200 depending on the state. In Indiana, the exact amount is set by the court on the citation (Indiana Bureau of Motor Vehicles).

We do not list a single Indiana dollar figure on this page. The $10–$200 range is a national spread across states, not a statement of what an Indiana court will order.

Your citation, or the court that issued it, is the only reliable source for what you owe. The amount can differ by county and court.

The fine is only one part of the bill. County court costs are added to the judgment, and those can change the total noticeably. The sections below explain how each piece works.

How is the seatbelt fine set in Indiana?

The court sets the fine within the limits of the state's traffic code. Indiana statutes are available through Indiana statutes via Justia, which is where the underlying provisions can be read in full.

That means two drivers cited for the same violation in different counties may see different totals. The citation itself, or the clerk's office for the court named on it, will show the amount.

Seatbelt rules also differ by state in who must buckle up and how the offense is enforced. The Governors Highway Safety Association keeps a state-by-state overview of these laws (Governors Highway Safety Association — State Laws).

  • The national range of $10–$200 is a comparison tool, not an Indiana quote.
  • The Indiana amount is set by the court on the citation.
  • Read the citation for the court name, the due date and how to respond.

Why can the total be higher than the fine?

County court costs are added to the judgment. For low-level violations, those costs often exceed the fine itself.

This matters for a seatbelt ticket, where the fine can be at the low end of the national range. The cost you pay may include more than the fine line on the citation.

We do not state a court-cost dollar amount because it varies by county and court. Ask the clerk for the total due, not just the fine.

Tip: when you call the clerk, ask for the full amount including court costs and any fees, and ask what payment methods and deadlines apply.

How many points does a seatbelt ticket carry in Indiana?

Indiana's points system assigns 2–8 points by mph over for speeding, and accumulating points triggers BMV probation and suspension (Indiana Bureau of Motor Vehicles). That scale is tied to speed, so it does not tell you what a seatbelt citation carries.

We do not state a point value for a seatbelt violation. Confirm with the BMV or the court whether your specific citation adds points to your record.

  • Points matter because accumulating them can lead to BMV probation and suspension.
  • Check your driving record through the BMV after the case closes.
  • If points do apply, a small ticket could still matter for your record over time.

Does a seatbelt ticket raise insurance?

Nationally, moving-violation convictions typically raise US premiums by about 20% on average. That is a national figure, not an Indiana-specific one.

Whether an insurer treats a seatbelt conviction the same way as a moving violation depends on the insurer and how your record reports it. We cannot say how your premium will change.

TicketCostGuide does not recommend insurers or give insurance advice. If you are concerned, ask your own insurer how a seatbelt citation is treated before you decide how to respond to it.

The ~20% figure is an average for moving-violation convictions across the US. Your own change could be higher, lower or none.

Can a seatbelt ticket be dismissed or reduced?

You generally have a few options when you receive a citation. Which ones are open to you depends on the court, so check the instructions on the citation.

  1. Pay the amount due. This usually resolves the case, but read the notice first so you know what paying means for your record.
  2. Contest it. You can ask for a hearing, where the court decides the outcome.
  3. Ask the clerk what is available. Some courts offer alternatives. We do not list any here, because we have no sourced figures or rules for them.

Because county court costs are added to the judgment, ask how any outcome affects the total owed, not only the fine.

TicketCostGuide is not a law firm and cannot give legal advice. For questions about your specific case, talk to a licensed attorney or the court clerk.

What happens if I don't pay a seatbelt ticket?

Ignoring a citation usually makes the situation worse. Courts set deadlines, and missing them can lead to further action on the case. The citation should state what applies to you.

Indiana's points system is enforced by the BMV, and accumulating points triggers probation and suspension. If your violation carries points, unresolved or unpaid matters may complicate your record.

  • Read the due date and court name on the citation as soon as you get it.
  • Contact the clerk before the deadline if you need more information.
  • Keep proof of payment or of your court date.
