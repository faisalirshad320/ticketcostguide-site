By Editorial Team, TicketCostGuide · Last reviewed 2026-10-16

Nationally, a seatbelt ticket runs $10–$200 for adults by state. In Arkansas the exact amount is set by the court on the citation (Arkansas Department of Finance and Administration — Driver Services). District courts also add court costs that vary by county, so totals can differ from place to place.

How much is a seatbelt ticket in Arkansas?

Nationally $10–$200 for adults by state; in Arkansas the exact amount is set by the court on the citation (Arkansas Department of Finance and Administration — Driver Services). The $10–$200 span describes how much states differ from one another. It is not a quote for an Arkansas ticket.

We do not print an exact Arkansas fine here, because the amount that applies to you is the one on your citation or in the court's records. The clerk of the court that issued the ticket can confirm what is owed.

The number you pay is usually a combination of two things:

  • The fine for the violation, set by the court within the state's traffic code.
  • Court costs added by the district court, which vary by county.

How is the fine for a seatbelt ticket set in Arkansas?

The exact fine for this violation in Arkansas is set by the court within the state's traffic code. The code itself is published through Arkansas statutes (Arkansas statutes via Justia), and the court applies it to your case.

That means the figure can depend on the court handling the citation, not just on the offense. Two people cited for the same thing in different counties may see different totals once costs are added.

For a national comparison of how states approach seatbelt rules, the Governors Highway Safety Association keeps a state-by-state reference (Governors Highway Safety Association — State Laws). Use it for context only. Your Arkansas citation controls what you owe.

Why is my total higher than the fine?

Court costs are added by the district court and vary by county. These costs sit on top of the fine, so the amount you pay at the window or online can be higher than the fine line on the citation.

We do not list a dollar amount for Arkansas court costs, since the amount varies and we cannot source one figure for every county. Ask the clerk for an itemized total before you pay.

Tip: compare the total due with the fine on the citation. The difference is typically court costs or other charges the clerk can itemize for you.

How many points does a seatbelt ticket carry in Arkansas?

The points system we sourced for Arkansas reads: 3–8 points by speed; 14+ points can trigger suspension. Those figures are tied to speed, so they describe speeding-type violations. We cannot confirm that a seatbelt citation adds any points, and we will not guess.

The Arkansas Department of Finance and Administration, Driver Services, is the place to check how a particular conviction is recorded on a driving record (Arkansas Department of Finance and Administration — Driver Services).

What matters most is the threshold. Points of any kind can add up, and 14+ points can trigger suspension. If you already have points on your record, ask Driver Services how a new conviction would count before deciding how to handle the ticket.

Does a seatbelt ticket raise insurance in Arkansas?

Moving-violation convictions typically raise US premiums about 20% on average. That is a national figure, not an Arkansas figure, and not a prediction for your policy.

Whether a seatbelt conviction is treated as a moving violation, or reported to your insurer at all, is something we cannot state from our sourced facts. Insurers set their own rules, and a company's rating practices can differ from the national average.

  • Treat 20% as a rough benchmark, not a quote.
  • Ask your insurer how it treats this kind of citation. TicketCostGuide does not recommend any insurer.
  • Check whether a conviction is actually entered on your record before assuming a rate change.

Can a seatbelt ticket be dismissed or reduced in Arkansas?

Because the court sets the amount on the citation, the court is also where any request about the ticket goes. Your citation should explain how to pay, how to contest, and where to appear.

We are not a law firm and cannot say whether your ticket qualifies for dismissal or a reduced amount. Options vary by court and county, so ask the clerk what is available in your case.

If you want legal advice about contesting a citation, speak with a licensed Arkansas attorney of your own choosing. We do not recommend specific attorneys.

Read the citation closely first. It lists the court, the amount due and the instructions that apply to your case.

What happens if I don't pay a seatbelt ticket?

Ignoring a citation is generally the worst option, because the court that issued it controls the next steps. We do not state specific deadlines or penalty amounts, since those are not part of our sourced figures and can vary by court.

Points matter here too. The state context we sourced says 14+ points can trigger suspension, so unresolved or additional convictions are worth taking seriously if you already carry points.

  • Find the court and the due date on the citation.
  • Contact the clerk if you cannot find the date or the amount.
  • Ask Driver Services how an unpaid or convicted ticket could affect your record.
