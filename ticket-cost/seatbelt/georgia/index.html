How much is a seatbelt ticket in Georgia?

Nationally, adult seatbelt fines run $10–$200 by state. In Georgia, the exact amount is set by the court on the citation (Georgia Department of Driver Services).

We do not list a single Georgia dollar figure on this page. The amount you owe depends on the court handling your case, and the citation or the court clerk is the reliable place to find it.

The $10–$200 range is a national range across states. Do not read it as the Georgia fine.

If you only have a ticket stub, look for the fine or amount-due line, the court name and the appearance or payment deadline. If any of those is missing, call the court listed on the citation.

How is a Georgia seatbelt fine set?

The fine for this violation is set by the court within the state's traffic code, not by the officer who writes the ticket. The officer records the violation and sends you to the court that will decide the amount.

That is why two people with the same violation can see different amounts on their paperwork. Court practices, local rules and your own case details can all play a part.

You can read Georgia's traffic code through Justia's Georgia statutes. The Governors Highway Safety Association also keeps a summary of seatbelt laws by state at GHSA State Laws.

  • Fine: set by the court on your citation.
  • Payment rules and deadlines: listed on the citation or by the court clerk.
  • Statute text: available through the Justia link above.

How many points does a seatbelt ticket carry in Georgia?

The Georgia points system we have on file is built around speed: 2 points for 15–18 mph over, 3 points for 19–23 over, 4 points for 24–33 over, and 6 points for 34+ over. Reaching 15 points in 24 months means a license suspension.

Those tiers are by mph over the limit, so they do not map directly onto a seatbelt violation. We cannot tell you from these figures how many points, if any, a seatbelt conviction adds.

To confirm how your specific citation is scored, check your driving record or contact Georgia DDS.

If you already have other tickets, count carefully. The 15-points-in-24-months threshold looks at your whole record, not one ticket.

Are there extra fees or surcharges on top of the fine?

Georgia adds a $200 Super Speeder state fee on top of the court fine. If it is not paid within 120 days, the license is suspended.

The name points to speeding-related cases. Nothing in our figures says this fee applies to a seatbelt ticket, so do not assume it does.

Court costs can also change the total you pay. Our figures do not give a Georgia amount for them, so ask the court what the full amount due is, not only the base fine.

  • Ask whether the amount on the citation is the total or only the base fine.
  • Ask whether any state fee applies to your violation.
  • Get the payment deadline in writing.

Does a seatbelt ticket raise insurance rates?

Moving-violation convictions typically raise US premiums about 20% on average. That is a national figure, not a Georgia-specific one.

Whether an insurer treats a seatbelt ticket as a moving violation, and how it prices it, depends on the company and your policy. Some insurers weigh it very differently from a speeding conviction.

We do not recommend insurers or predict your premium. If you are concerned, ask your insurer how it treats this kind of citation before you decide how to resolve it.

The ~20% figure is an average across many violations and drivers. Your own change could be higher, lower or none.

Can a seatbelt ticket be dismissed or reduced?

That is decided by the court handling your citation. Our figures do not show which outcomes are available in a given Georgia court, so we cannot promise any result.

Your practical options usually look like this:

  1. Pay the amount the court sets on the citation.
  2. Contact the court clerk to ask what resolution options exist for your case.
  3. Contest the citation at the court date listed.
  4. Talk with a licensed attorney if you want legal advice about your situation.

TicketCostGuide is not a law firm and does not give legal advice or recommend an attorney. Before paying, find out whether paying counts as a conviction on your record, because that can matter for insurance.

What happens if I don't pay a Georgia seatbelt ticket?

The court sets the deadlines and the penalties for late payment, and they are printed on your citation or available from the clerk. We do not list those penalties here.

One consequence we can state: the $200 Super Speeder state fee must be paid within 120 days or the license is suspended. That applies to that fee, and we have no basis to say it applies to a seatbelt ticket.

Separately, 15 points in 24 months leads to suspension under the points system. Ignoring a ticket can lead to bigger problems than the original fine, so contact the court before the deadline passes.

For official license-status questions, use Georgia DDS.
