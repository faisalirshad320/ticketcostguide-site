How much is a driving without a license ticket in Maine?

Nationally, a first offense for driving without a license commonly runs $50–$500, and it is often chargeable as a misdemeanor. That is a national range, not a Maine price list (Maine Bureau of Motor Vehicles).

In Maine, the exact amount is set by the court on the citation. We do not publish a single Maine dollar figure because the amount depends on the charge written and on the court handling it.

Bottom line: use $50–$500 as a rough national frame only. The number that applies to you is the one the court sets on your citation.

Statutory surcharges are built into the scheduled amounts. That means the total you owe should already account for them, rather than being a surprise add-on, but confirm this against your own paperwork.

How is the fine set for driving without a license in Maine?

The fine for this violation is set by the court within the state's traffic code. The statutes are available through Maine statutes via Justia, and the section named on your citation is the best guide to what you were charged with.

Several things can change the outcome from one case to another:

  • The specific charge written on the citation.
  • Whether the offense is treated as a misdemeanor or a lesser violation.
  • Whether it is a first offense or a repeat.
  • The court that hears the case.

"Driving without a license" can describe different situations, such as never having been licensed or driving on a license that is no longer valid. The charge name and statute on your citation tell you which one applies. Our locked facts do not break out separate Maine amounts for each, so we do not guess at them.

How many points does driving without a license add in Maine?

Maine uses a demerit point system. Points accrue per conviction, and reaching 12 points in 12 months results in a suspension.

We do not state a specific point value for this offense, because it is not among the figures we have verified. Whether a conviction adds points at all, and how many, should be checked against the citation and with the Maine Bureau of Motor Vehicles.

One practical note: if you do not currently hold a license, points may matter mainly later, when you apply for one. A conviction can still show on your record. The BMV is the right place to ask how a conviction would be treated when you apply.

Does driving without a license raise insurance rates?

Nationally, moving-violation convictions typically raise US premiums by about 20% on average. That is a national figure, not a Maine-specific one, and individual results vary by insurer and driving history.

Whether a no-license conviction counts the same way as a speeding conviction depends on the insurer's own rules. Some people in this situation have no policy in their name, which changes the question.

TicketCostGuide does not recommend any insurer. If you have a policy, the neutral step is to ask your insurer how it treats this type of conviction. You can also review general state driving rules at Governors Highway Safety Association — State Laws.

Can a driving without a license ticket be dismissed or reduced in Maine?

Possibly, but outcomes are decided by the court, and we cannot predict them. This page is not legal advice.

The general options people have after a citation include:

  • Paying the amount as written, which usually resolves the case but may count as a conviction.
  • Contesting the charge at the court date listed on the citation.
  • Asking the court clerk what is allowed in your situation, such as payment arrangements.
  • Consulting a licensed attorney of your own choosing, especially because this offense is often chargeable as a misdemeanor.

If the offense is a misdemeanor, the stakes can be higher than for a basic traffic infraction. Read the citation closely for the charge, the statute and the deadline before you decide anything.

Paying a citation is generally treated as accepting it. If you are unsure, ask the court before you pay.

What happens if I don't pay or ignore the citation?

Ignoring a citation tends to make things worse. We do not list specific Maine late fees or deadlines here because they are not in our verified figures, so check your citation and the court for exact terms.

In general, unresolved citations can lead to additional court action. Because the Maine traffic code ties points and suspensions to convictions, an unpaid or unresolved matter can also complicate your driving record.

If you miss a date, contact the court clerk as soon as you can and ask what steps remain open to you. Acting early usually leaves more options than waiting.
