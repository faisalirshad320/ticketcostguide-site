How much is a red light ticket in Virginia?

The honest short answer is that the amount depends on your citation. Nationally, red light tickets run $50–$500 depending on the state. In Virginia, the exact amount is set by the court on the citation (Virginia Department of Motor Vehicles).

We do not publish a single Virginia dollar figure here because the sourced facts do not support one. The national range is context only. It is not a Virginia price.

Your citation is the best source for your amount. Check the fine, the court name and the deadline printed on it.

If your ticket lists a court appearance rather than a fixed prepayment amount, the court may decide the final amount. Contact the court clerk listed on the citation to confirm what you owe.

How is the red light fine set in Virginia?

The fine for this violation is set by the court within the state's traffic code. It is not a flat number that every driver pays in every county. The statutes are collected in the Virginia statutes via Justia database if you want to read the code itself.

Two things can change what you see on paper:

  • The court that handles your citation.
  • Whether the case is treated as an ordinary violation or as a reckless case.

Because the amount is set by the court, two drivers with similar tickets can see different totals. Always rely on your own citation rather than a number from a website, including this one.

Are there extra court costs on top of the fine?

Yes. Fixed processing costs apply to every prepaid ticket in Virginia. That means the amount you pay is not only the fine itself.

Reckless cases are different. They add court-set fines and fees on top of the processing costs. A reckless charge can therefore cost more than an ordinary red light violation.

We do not list a dollar value for the processing costs because the locked facts do not give one. The court clerk or the payment instructions on your citation should show the total due.

Before paying, confirm whether the amount shown is the fine alone or the total including processing costs.

How many points does a red light ticket add in Virginia?

Virginia uses a demerit point system. Offenses carry 3–6 demerit points depending on the offense, and reckless offenses carry 6 (Virginia Department of Motor Vehicles).

The exact point value for your ticket depends on how the offense is charged and on whether there is a conviction. Check the charge on your citation, and check your record with the DMV if you are unsure.

Two rules matter for your license:

  • The DMV suspends a license when point thresholds are reached.
  • Drivers earn +1 safe point for each clean year.

We do not state the threshold numbers here because they are not part of our locked facts. The DMV is the authority on them.

Does a red light ticket raise insurance in Virginia?

It can. Moving-violation convictions typically raise US premiums about 20% on average. That is a national figure, not a Virginia quote.

Your own result depends on your insurer, your driving history and how the violation is recorded. We do not recommend any insurer and cannot predict your rate.

If you want to know how a conviction might affect you, ask your insurance company how it treats moving violations. Asking in general terms does not commit you to anything.

The 20% figure is an average. Some drivers may see less change and some more.

Can a red light ticket be dismissed or reduced?

That is a question for the court. The fine is set by the court, so the court is where any change would happen.

In general, drivers with a citation can consider these paths:

  1. Pay the ticket, which is handled as a prepaid ticket with fixed processing costs.
  2. Contest the citation in court by following the instructions printed on it.
  3. Ask the court clerk what procedures apply to your case.

Paying is usually treated as accepting the charge, which can matter for points and insurance. Contesting means appearing in court and may involve more time.

What happens if I don't pay a red light ticket?

Ignoring a citation tends to make the situation worse. The court may add court-set fines and fees, and your license can be affected.

The citation lists a deadline and a court. If you cannot meet the deadline, contact the clerk before it passes rather than after.

Points matter here too. Because the DMV suspends licenses at point thresholds, a conviction adds to your total. Clean years then earn +1 safe point each, which helps over time.

We do not list specific late fees because the locked facts do not include any. The court can tell you what applies to your case.

Can I get points removed in Virginia?

Drivers earn +1 safe point for each clean year, which offsets demerit points over time. Beyond that, we do not list other removal options. The Virginia DMV is the best source for how your record is calculated and what applies to you.
