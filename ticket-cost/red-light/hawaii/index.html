How much is a red light ticket in Hawaii?

Nationally, a red light ticket costs $50–$500 depending on the state. That is a national range, not a Hawaii price. In Hawaii, the exact amount is set by the court on the citation (Hawaii Department of Transportation).

This page does not quote a specific Hawaii dollar figure, because the amount you owe is the one printed on your own citation or shown by the court handling your case.

Your citation is the most reliable source for what you owe. If the number there differs from anything you read online, go with the citation and the court's instructions.

Keep in mind that the fine is only part of the cost. Court fees and assessments are added by the district court, and a conviction can also affect what you pay for car insurance later.

How is the fine for running a red light set in Hawaii?

In Hawaii, the exact fine for this violation is set by the court within the state's traffic code. You do not choose the amount, and an officer's roadside comment is not the final word. The figure on the citation or the court's notice is what counts.

The traffic code itself is published in the Hawaii statutes, which you can read through Hawaii statutes via Justia. Look for the traffic and vehicle provisions if you want the exact wording of the offense you were cited for.

Because the court sets the amount, two things are worth checking on your paperwork:

  • The violation described, to confirm it is the red light offense you remember.
  • The total due, to see whether it includes court fees and assessments.

If anything on the citation looks wrong or unclear, the court clerk can explain the process. The clerk cannot give legal advice, but can tell you how the court handles payment and hearings.

What court costs and surcharges are added?

Court fees and assessments are added by the district court. These sit on top of the base fine, so the total you pay can be higher than the fine alone.

We do not list exact surcharge amounts for Hawaii, because they are applied by the court and we have no sourced figure to publish. The court's notice should show the full amount due.

When you compare what you might pay, use the total owed, not just the fine line. A lower fine with added assessments can cost about the same as a higher fine with fewer add-ons.

How many points does a red light ticket add in Hawaii?

None, because Hawaii has no demerit-point system. Many states count points toward a suspension threshold, but Hawaii does not work that way.

Instead, repeat convictions and excessive-speeding convictions drive license action directly. In other words, the risk to your license comes from your conviction record, not from a running point total.

For a single red light ticket, this means there is no point count to track. A conviction still goes on your record, and repeat convictions matter. You can compare state approaches on the Governors Highway Safety Association state laws page.

Does a red light ticket raise insurance in Hawaii?

It can. Moving-violation convictions typically raise US premiums about 20% on average. That is a national figure, not a Hawaii-specific one.

Whether your own rate changes depends on your insurer, your driving history and how the ticket is resolved. We do not know your policy terms and cannot predict your premium.

  • Ask your insurer how it treats a conviction for a moving violation.
  • Ask when your next renewal review happens.
  • Keep a copy of how the case was resolved, in case it is questioned later.

Because a premium increase can last beyond the ticket itself, many drivers weigh the long-term insurance effect alongside the fine when deciding how to respond.

Can a red light ticket be dismissed or reduced?

Drivers generally have a few paths: pay the citation, contest it, or ask the court what other options exist. The citation and court notice should explain which are available to you and by when.

Paying usually resolves the case quickly, but it can mean accepting the violation. Contesting means presenting your side to the court. Whether a case can be reduced or dismissed is up to the court, not us.

TicketCostGuide is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. If the outcome matters for your license or job, consider speaking with a licensed Hawaii attorney of your own choosing.

Whatever you choose, act within the deadline printed on your paperwork. Missing it can limit your options.

What happens if I don't pay a red light ticket in Hawaii?

Ignoring a citation rarely makes it go away. Because the court sets the amount and adds its own fees and assessments, an unresolved case can grow more complicated, and the court may take further steps.

Since repeat convictions drive license action directly in Hawaii, it is worth resolving each citation properly rather than letting it lapse. We cannot say exactly what steps a particular court will take, so read the notice on your citation.

If you cannot pay the full amount or cannot make a date, contact the court clerk before the deadline passes and ask what options exist.
