How much is a red light ticket in Georgia?

Nationally, red light tickets run $50–$500 depending on the state. In Georgia, the exact amount is set by the court on the citation (Georgia Department of Driver Services).

This guide does not publish a single Georgia dollar figure. The amount depends on the court handling your case, and the number on your citation or court notice is the one that counts.

The $50–$500 range is a national spread across states. Do not treat it as the Georgia fine.

Your total can also include extra items on top of the base fine. The next sections cover the fee and points details we can source.

How is a Georgia red light fine set?

The fine for this violation is set by the court within the state's traffic code. The code is published online, and Georgia statutes can be read through Justia.

Because the court sets the amount, two drivers cited for the same thing can see different totals depending on where the case is handled. The citation or the court's notice is the best place to find your amount.

  • Check the citation for the court name and any listed amount.
  • Look at the court's own notice or website for payment instructions.
  • Ask the clerk's office what the total includes if the figure looks higher than expected.

We do not estimate your amount, because any estimate would be a guess.

How many points is a red light ticket in Georgia?

Georgia's points system runs from 2 to 6 points, scaled by mph over the limit: 2 points for 15–18 over, 3 points for 19–23 over, 4 points for 24–33 over, and 6 points for 34 or more over.

That scale is built around speed. Our sources do not list a separate point value for a red light violation, so we do not state one. To see how a specific conviction is treated, check your driving record and the guidance at Georgia DDS.

The suspension threshold is clear: 15 points in 24 months = suspension. Any points you already have matter when a new ticket is added.

Before paying, find out whether your conviction would add points. Paying a ticket is generally treated as a guilty plea.

Are there extra state fees on top of the fine?

Georgia has a $200 Super Speeder state fee that is added on top of the court fine. If it is not paid within 120 days, the license is suspended.

The fee is tied to the Super Speeder program, which concerns speeding. Our sources do not say it applies to a red light citation by itself, so confirm with the court or DDS whether any state fee applies to your case.

If a fee does appear on your paperwork, note its deadline separately from the fine's due date. A missed fee deadline can cause a suspension even when the fine itself is paid.

Does a red light ticket raise insurance in Georgia?

It can. Moving-violation convictions typically raise US premiums by about 20% on average. That is a national figure, not a Georgia-specific rate.

What you pay depends on your insurer, your record and how the violation is reported. We do not recommend any insurer, and we cannot predict your premium.

  • Premium changes often show up at renewal, not right away.
  • A conviction usually matters more to rates than a ticket that did not result in one.
  • Your insurer can tell you how it treats a given violation.

When comparing options, count the possible premium increase as part of the ticket's cost, alongside the fine.

Can a red light ticket be dismissed or reduced?

Sometimes the outcome can change, but that depends on the court and the facts of your case. We cannot say what is available to you or how likely it is.

In general, a driver has a few paths:

  1. Pay the ticket. This usually ends the case but is treated as a conviction, with the cost, points and insurance effects described above.
  2. Contest it. You can ask the court how to dispute the citation and what the process involves.
  3. Ask the court about alternatives. Some courts offer other ways to resolve a ticket. The clerk can say whether yours does.

TicketCostGuide is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. If you want advice on your case, you can speak with a licensed Georgia attorney of your choosing.

What happens if I don't pay a red light ticket in Georgia?

Ignoring a ticket can make things worse. The one consequence we can source: failure to pay the $200 Super Speeder state fee within 120 days suspends the license.

Courts may take further steps on unpaid or ignored citations, and the details vary by court. The notice you received should state your deadline and what happens if it passes.

If you cannot pay by the due date, contact the court before the deadline instead of waiting. A suspension or added consequences are usually harder to undo than to prevent.

A suspended license can also put you closer to trouble if you are already carrying points. The threshold is 15 points in 24 months.
