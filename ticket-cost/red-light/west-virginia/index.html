How much is a red light ticket in West Virginia?

Nationally, red light tickets fall in a range of $50–$500 depending on the state. In West Virginia, the exact amount is set by the court on the citation (West Virginia Division of Motor Vehicles).

We do not list a single West Virginia dollar figure here, because the amount depends on the court that hears your case. The national range is context only. It is not a West Virginia price list.

Your citation is the best source for your number. Look for the fine, any listed court costs, and the court name and address.

If the paperwork shows only a court appearance and no amount, the court clerk can tell you what is owed. Ask for the total, not just the fine.

How is the fine for running a red light set in West Virginia?

The fine is not a flat statewide price. It is set by the court within the state's traffic code, which you can read through West Virginia statutes via Justia.

That means two drivers cited for the same offense can see different totals depending on which court handles the case and what the judge or magistrate decides.

  • The statute sets the framework the court works within.
  • The court sets the amount that appears on your citation.
  • The citation is where you confirm what you owe.

Do not rely on a figure from another state or an online average. Those numbers can be far from what a West Virginia court assigns.

Why can court costs be more than the fine?

Municipal and magistrate court costs vary and usually exceed the fine. So the fine line on the ticket is often only part of the bill.

When you compare options, compare the total payable, not just the fine. A ticket that looks modest can cost more once court costs are added.

  • Check whether the amount shown includes court costs.
  • Ask the clerk for a written total before you pay.
  • Keep the receipt and the case number.

Because costs vary by court, a neighbor's total may not match yours, even for a similar ticket.

How many points does a red light ticket add in West Virginia?

West Virginia's points system assigns 2–5 points by mph over, and 12+ points = suspension. That description is built around speed, so it does not tell you the exact point value for a red light violation.

Confirm the point value for your specific violation with the West Virginia Division of Motor Vehicles. The DMV keeps your driving record and can tell you how a conviction is recorded.

Why points matter: reaching 12+ points means suspension. Points from several tickets can add up, so one red light conviction is worth checking against your existing record.

A general overview of how states handle traffic laws is available from the Governors Highway Safety Association — State Laws.

Does a red light ticket raise insurance rates?

It can. Moving-violation convictions typically raise US premiums by about 20% on average. That is a national figure, not a West Virginia-specific one.

Your own change could be higher, lower, or nothing at all. It depends on your insurer, your driving history, and how the ticket is resolved.

  • A conviction is more likely to affect premiums than a dismissed ticket.
  • Rate changes often show up at renewal, not right away.
  • Your insurer can tell you how it treats a moving violation. We do not recommend any insurer.

When weighing whether to pay or contest, include the possible premium increase alongside the fine and court costs.

Can a red light ticket be dismissed or reduced?

Sometimes, but it depends on the court and the facts. We cannot tell you whether your ticket qualifies, and this page is not legal advice.

Your citation will list how to respond. Typical choices are:

  1. Pay the ticket. This usually counts as a conviction, which can bring points and an insurance review.
  2. Contest it. You ask the court to hear the case. Costs may apply, and the outcome is not guaranteed.
  3. Ask the court about alternatives. Some courts offer other ways to resolve a ticket. Ask the clerk what is available.

If you are unsure, a licensed West Virginia attorney can review your situation. We do not recommend a specific attorney.

What happens if I don't pay a red light ticket?

Ignoring the citation usually makes things worse. The court can add consequences, and an unresolved ticket can put your license at risk.

West Virginia's points system includes suspension at 12+ points. Separately, unpaid or ignored tickets can lead to other court action. Check the exact consequences with the court that issued the citation.

If you cannot pay the full amount, contact the court clerk before the deadline on your citation. Ask what options exist.

Always read the deadline and instructions on the ticket itself. They are specific to your court.
