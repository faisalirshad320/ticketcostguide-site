How much is a stop sign ticket in Georgia?

Nationally, stop sign tickets run $50–$500 depending on the state. In Georgia, the exact amount is set by the court on the citation (Georgia Department of Driver Services (DDS)).

We do not state a single Georgia dollar figure here, because the amount is not one fixed number. The national range is a benchmark for comparison, not a price quote for your ticket.

The most reliable number is the one printed on your citation or shown by the court that issued it. If neither lists an amount, contact that court's clerk.

Court costs and state fees can change the total you actually pay, so ask what the full amount due is, not only the base fine.

How is the fine for a stop sign violation set in Georgia?

In Georgia, the court sets the fine within the state's traffic code. That is why two drivers cited for the same violation can see different amounts depending on the court handling the case.

The statutes are published online through Georgia statutes via Justia. They describe the offense and the legal framework, but the amount you owe is the one the court assigns on your citation.

  • Start with the citation. It names the court, the charge and any appearance or payment instructions.
  • Check the court. The clerk can tell you what is owed and what payment options exist.
  • Ask about added costs. The base fine is not always the full amount due.

How many points is a stop sign ticket in Georgia?

Georgia's points system runs from 2 to 6 points. The schedule we have is written by miles per hour over the limit: 2 points for 15–18 over, 3 points for 19–23 over, 4 points for 24–33 over, and 6 points for 34 or more over.

Because that schedule is about speed, it does not give a stop sign value. We will not guess one. Check your citation and the Georgia Department of Driver Services (DDS) to see whether and how points apply to your conviction.

The suspension threshold is 15 points in 24 months. Points from any violations count toward that total, so your existing record matters.

Are there extra state fees on top of the fine?

Georgia adds a $200 Super Speeder state fee on top of the court fine. If it is not paid within 120 days, the license is suspended.

The name points to a speeding-related fee, not a stop sign charge. Do not assume it applies to your stop sign ticket, and do not assume it does not. Check the citation and ask the court or DDS.

If a fee does apply to you, track its deadline separately from the court's payment date. A missed state-fee deadline can lead to a suspension even when the fine itself is paid.

Does a stop sign ticket raise insurance in Georgia?

It can. Moving-violation convictions typically raise US premiums by about 20% on average. That is a national figure, not a Georgia-specific or insurer-specific rate.

Your actual change depends on your insurer, your driving record and how the violation is recorded. A conviction on your record is what usually matters most to an insurer.

  • Ask your insurer how it treats a stop sign conviction before you decide how to respond.
  • Compare the possible premium increase with the cost of the ticket itself.
  • Remember that a premium change can last well beyond the ticket's due date.

TicketCostGuide does not recommend any insurer.

Can a stop sign ticket be dismissed or reduced in Georgia?

Possibly, but the outcome is decided by the court, and we cannot predict it. Your citation lists the court, and the clerk can explain the choices open to you, such as paying or contesting.

Paying the citation generally resolves the case but may mean a conviction on your record. Contesting it means appearing before the court. Each path affects your points and insurance differently.

This guide is not legal advice. If you are weighing a contest, consider speaking with a licensed attorney of your choosing.

Before deciding, compare the cost of the fine with your point total and the national insurance effect of about 20%.

What happens if I don't pay a stop sign ticket in Georgia?

Ignoring a citation can make things worse. The two risks we can state from our sources are tied to fees and points.

  • If the $200 Super Speeder state fee applies and is not paid within 120 days, the license is suspended.
  • Reaching 15 points in 24 months also leads to suspension.

A court may also take its own steps for an unpaid or unanswered citation. We do not list those here because they depend on the court. Contact the court named on your citation as soon as possible.
