How much is a reckless driving ticket in Kentucky?

Nationally, reckless driving fines commonly run up to $1,000–$2,500, and the offense is a criminal misdemeanor with possible jail (Kentucky Transportation Cabinet). That range is a national figure. It is not a Kentucky price list.

In Kentucky, the exact amount is set by the court on the citation. We do not state a single Kentucky dollar figure because the court decides it within the state's traffic code.

Read the citation itself. It, or the court that handles it, is the authority on what you owe in Kentucky.

The fine is also only part of the bill. Court costs are added, and they are covered below.

How is a reckless driving fine set in Kentucky?

The fine for this violation is set by the court within the state's traffic code. A judge or the court's schedule determines the figure, and it appears on the citation. Kentucky statutes are available through Kentucky statutes via Justia if you want to read the traffic code yourself.

Reckless driving is a criminal misdemeanor, which is different from many ordinary moving violations. That means the court has more room to consider the facts of the stop. Jail is possible, not automatic.

Several things can shape the outcome in any state:

  • The facts described by the officer on the citation
  • Whether anyone was hurt or property was damaged
  • Your driving record
  • How the court handles your plea and appearance

We cannot predict how a particular judge will rule. We can only point to what the court sets and where to check it.

What are court costs, and do they add to the fine?

Yes. Court costs are set by the district court and usually exceed the fine itself. A total bill can therefore be noticeably larger than the fine line on the citation.

Because costs are set by the court, they are not one fixed statewide number in our sources. We do not give a dollar figure for them.

When you ask the court clerk what you owe, ask for the total, meaning the fine plus all costs and surcharges, not just the fine.

This matters if you are planning to pay or arranging a payment. Budgeting only for the fine can leave you short.

How many points does reckless driving carry in Kentucky?

Our locked Kentucky points facts are: 3 points (10–15 over) or 6 points (16–25 over); 26+ over triggers a hearing; 12 points in 2 years = suspension. These tiers are written around how far over a limit a driver was.

They do not state a separate point value for reckless driving. We will not guess one. Confirm the points tied to your specific conviction with the Kentucky Transportation Cabinet, which handles driver licensing.

  • 3 points: 10–15 over
  • 6 points: 16–25 over
  • 26+ over: triggers a hearing
  • 12 points in 2 years: suspension

The suspension rule is the one to watch. Points from several tickets within two years add up toward the 12-point line.

Does reckless driving raise insurance rates?

It can. Moving-violation convictions typically raise US premiums about 20% on average. That is a national figure, not a Kentucky rate, and it is for moving violations in general.

Reckless driving is a criminal misdemeanor, so the effect on an individual policy may differ from an ordinary ticket. We do not have a locked figure for it, and we do not recommend any insurer. Your own insurer's rules decide what happens to your rate.

The 20% average also describes a typical change, not a promise. Some drivers see less, some see more, and some face a different outcome at renewal.

Can a reckless driving ticket be dismissed or reduced?

Sometimes outcomes change, but our sources do not set out Kentucky rules for dismissal or reduction, so we do not describe specific programs. What we can say is that the court sets the fine on the citation, and the case goes through the district court.

Because this is a criminal misdemeanor with possible jail, many people choose to speak with a licensed attorney. TicketCostGuide is not a law firm, gives no legal advice and does not recommend any attorney.

Practical steps that do not require legal advice:

  1. Read the citation for the court, the charge and any date.
  2. Contact the court clerk to confirm the total amount owed.
  3. Check your points status with the Kentucky Transportation Cabinet.
  4. Ask your insurer how a conviction would be treated.

What happens if I don't pay or ignore it?

Ignoring a criminal misdemeanor citation is risky. Courts generally treat a missed appearance or unpaid amount as a further problem, and the matter can grow beyond the original fine. Our locked sources do not list Kentucky's specific penalties for non-payment, so we do not state them.

Your licence is a separate concern. In Kentucky, 12 points in 2 years = suspension, so a conviction that adds points can move you closer to that threshold.

If you cannot pay in full, contact the court clerk before the deadline on your citation and ask what options exist.

Court costs, which usually exceed the fine, are part of what you owe, so include them when you plan.
