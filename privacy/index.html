TicketCostGuide ("we") operates ticketcostguide.com. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the site.

What we collect

We do not require accounts and do not ask for personal information. Standard server logs (IP address, user agent, pages requested) are kept for security and aggregate traffic measurement.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve ads. Google and its partners may use cookies or device identifiers to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. Calculator inputs are processed entirely in your browser and are not transmitted to or stored by us.
