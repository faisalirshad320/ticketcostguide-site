How much is a speeding ticket in Colorado?

For speeding 1–24 mph over the limit, the Colorado penalty schedule puts fines at roughly $36–$232 plus surcharges (Colorado Division of Motor Vehicles). The speeding offense itself is set out in C.R.S. §42-4-1101.

That range is a fine only. A statutory surcharge is added to each penalty, so the amount you owe will be higher than the base fine.

The exact total on your ticket depends on your county or court, so the number printed on your citation is the one to rely on.

Quick math: the fine from the penalty schedule + the statutory surcharge = what you owe on a payable ticket. Insurance costs come on top of that and are not part of the fine.

What drives the total cost of a Colorado speeding ticket?

Several things move the number up or down. The biggest is how many miles per hour you were over the limit, because the penalty schedule scales the fine with speed.

  • Speed over the limit: the schedule runs roughly $36–$232 for 1–24 over.
  • Surcharges: a statutory surcharge is added to each penalty, and its amount varies by county and court.
  • Work zones: doubled fines apply in work zones.
  • Severity: at 25+ over, the case is a criminal traffic matter and the court sets the fines.

Points and insurance are separate from the ticket price but affect what the ticket costs you over time.

Are fines higher in work zones?

Yes. Doubled fines apply in work zones, which means a speed that would carry one fine on an ordinary road can cost considerably more in a marked work zone.

The locked penalty-schedule range of roughly $36–$232 is the standard schedule. We do not give a separate doubled dollar figure because it depends on the underlying fine, and the surcharge is a separate item that varies by county and court.

If your citation notes a work zone, read it closely and confirm the amount with the court listed on it.

How many points does a speeding ticket add in Colorado?

A Colorado speeding conviction adds 1–6 points, depending on how many miles per hour over the limit you were. Faster speeds mean more points.

The threshold that matters most is 12 points in 12 months for an adult driver, which results in a suspension (Colorado Division of Motor Vehicles).

  • Points are assigned by mph over the limit.
  • Points on your record are separate from the fine and surcharge.
  • A single high-speed ticket and several smaller ones can both lead toward the 12-point mark.

For a view of how other states handle traffic penalties, see the Governors Highway Safety Association's state laws resource.

What happens if I'm 25+ mph over the limit?

At 25+ mph over the limit, the case becomes a criminal traffic matter rather than a payable ticket. It is a misdemeanor traffic offense with court-set fines.

In practice that means you cannot treat it like a standard ticket and simply pay a scheduled amount. The court decides the fines, and the outcome is not drawn from the roughly $36–$232 schedule that applies to 1–24 over.

TicketCostGuide is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. If your ticket is in this tier, read the paperwork carefully and contact the court named on it about what is required.

Does a speeding ticket raise insurance in Colorado?

It can. Published market analyses show that a speeding conviction typically raises US auto premiums by roughly 20–25% on average at renewal.

That is a national average, not a Colorado figure. Your own change depends on your insurer, your driving history and the conviction itself, so it may be higher or lower.

Because the premium effect can last across renewals, it may end up costing more than the fine itself. We do not recommend any insurer, and you should ask your own provider how a conviction would affect your policy.

Can a Colorado speeding ticket be reduced, and what if I don't pay?

Whether a ticket can be dismissed or reduced is decided by the court and prosecutor handling your case. We do not state specific outcomes or programs here, because they vary by county and court.

Your citation lists the court and the steps to respond. Following those instructions is the first thing to do.

Ignoring a ticket is risky. With a payable ticket, unpaid amounts and missed court dates can lead to further consequences, and a 25+ over case is a criminal traffic matter where the court sets what happens next. Our sources do not give specific penalties for non-payment, so check with the court named on your citation.

Since points and a suspension threshold of 12 points in 12 months (adult) are also in play, a conviction can have effects beyond the fine.

Frequently asked questions

How many points is a speeding ticket in Colorado?

How many points will suspend my license in Colorado?

Are speeding fines doubled in Colorado work zones?

Yes. Doubled fines apply in work zones. The standard penalty schedule for 1–24 mph over is roughly $36–$232 plus surcharges, and a work-zone citation can cost more. Check your citation or the court for the exact amount owed.

What happens if I'm going 25 mph or more over the limit in Colorado?

Is the surcharge included in the fine range?
