How much is a speeding ticket in Nebraska?

The lead figure is statutory fines of $10–$300 by mph-over tier, plus court costs of roughly $50 (Nebraska Department of Motor Vehicles, last reviewed Oct 2026). The fine portion comes from the tiers in Neb. Rev. Stat. §60-682.01.

The low end of the range applies to the smallest overages. The high end applies to the most serious speeds. Where your ticket falls depends on how far over the limit you were cited.

Nebraska's tiers are set in statute, so the bracket on the ticket fixes the fine. Court costs are then added on top.

Treat the range as a guide to the fine and add the court costs to estimate what you would owe. The amount printed on your citation or shown by the court is the one that counts.

What drives the total on a Nebraska speeding ticket?

Three things shape the final amount: the mph-over tier, whether the location carries a doubled fine, and the court costs added to the case.

  • The mph-over tier. This sets the base fine within the $10–$300 range.
  • The zone. Fines are doubled in some zones, which raises the base fine.
  • Court costs. These are added statewide, at roughly $50, and the exact amount can vary by county or court.

Because the tiers are in statute, there is little room for the amount to move once the officer writes the bracket. The main variables left are the zone and the court's added costs.

Check the citation for the offense description and the listed speed. If the tier on the ticket does not match what you recall, that is something to raise with the court clerk or a licensed attorney, not something this guide can resolve.

Are Nebraska speeding fines doubled in some zones?

Yes. Our locked sourcing for this page states that fines are doubled in some zones. We do not list which zones or give a doubled dollar figure here, because we have not verified those details to a published source.

The practical point is that the same speed can cost more in a zone with a doubling rule than on an ordinary stretch of road. Look at signs and the citation itself.

The statute is Neb. Rev. Stat. §60-682.01. Read the current text, or ask the court, to confirm how doubling applies to your ticket.

Court costs are a separate add-on. They are added statewide and are not the same thing as a doubled fine.

How many points does a speeding ticket add in Nebraska?

A speeding conviction adds 1–3 points, depending on how many miles per hour over the limit you were going. Faster speeds fall into higher point tiers.

Points matter because of the revocation threshold: 12 points in 2 years leads to revocation. A single speeding ticket will not reach that on its own, but points from several violations can add up.

  • Points per ticket: 1–3, by mph over.
  • Revocation threshold: 12 points in 2 years.

For your own point total and license status, the Nebraska Department of Motor Vehicles is the place to check. Out-of-state drivers should also ask their home state how a Nebraska conviction is reported.

Does a Nebraska speeding ticket raise insurance?

It can. Published market analyses indicate that a speeding conviction typically raises US auto premiums by roughly 20–25% on average at renewal.

That is a national average, not a Nebraska figure. Your own change could be smaller, larger or nil, depending on your insurer, your record and how the violation is reported.

Because that increase often lasts several renewals, it can exceed the fine itself over time. We do not name insurers or recommend one. If you are worried about a rate change, ask your own insurer how it treats a conviction of this kind.

Compare the one-time fine and court costs with a possible renewal increase before deciding how to handle the ticket.

Can a Nebraska speeding ticket be dismissed or reduced?

You can contest a citation in court, and the outcome is decided by the court, not by this guide. We cannot say how likely a dismissal or reduction is, and we do not give legal advice.

Since the fine follows the bracket on the ticket, a lower fine generally means the case ends in a different tier or a different charge. Whether that is available in your case is a question for the court or a licensed attorney of your choosing.

  • Read the citation for the stated speed and location.
  • Note the court and any deadlines printed on it.
  • Ask the clerk what options and costs apply.

Remember that court costs of roughly $50 are added statewide, so even a successful outcome may involve some cost.

What happens if I don't pay a Nebraska speeding ticket?

Ignoring a ticket usually makes things worse, but the specific penalties and deadlines are not in our locked sources, so we do not list them here. The citation and the court clerk will give the dates that apply to you.

In general, an unresolved ticket can lead to added consequences from the court and can affect your driving record. Points on your record count toward the 12 points in 2 years revocation threshold.

If you cannot pay in full, contact the court named on the citation before the deadline and ask what is allowed. Payment rules vary by court.
