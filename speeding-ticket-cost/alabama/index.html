How Much Does a Speeding Ticket Cost in Alabama? Fines, Court Costs and Points

By Editorial Team, TicketCostGuide · Last reviewed 2026-10-15

A speeding ticket in Alabama typically costs $20–$200 base fine, plus court costs that vary by municipality (Alabama Law Enforcement Agency). Court costs often exceed the base fine, so the total depends on the court handling your citation. Points: 2 or 5, depending on how far over the limit.

How much is a speeding ticket in Alabama?

The base fine for a speeding ticket in Alabama is typically $20–$200, plus court costs that vary by municipality (Alabama Law Enforcement Agency). That range is the fine before any court costs are added.

The statute that governs speed limits and the penalties for exceeding them is Ala. Code §32-5A-171 (Justia). The code sets the framework, and the local court that handles your citation sets the actual fine within those limits.

Because of that, two drivers cited for similar speeds can owe different totals. We do not publish a single statewide figure, because none exists.

Bottom line: the base fine is only part of the bill. Municipal and district court costs vary by jurisdiction and often exceed the base fine.

What drives the total cost of an Alabama speeding ticket?

Several factors move the final number. Most of them come down to which court has your case.

  • The court: Fines are set within statutory limits by the local court handling the citation, so totals vary widely by municipality.
  • Court costs: Municipal and district court costs vary by jurisdiction and often exceed the base fine.
  • How far over the limit you were: This affects the points added to your record (2 or 5), and the court may weigh it when setting the fine.
  • Insurance at renewal: This is not part of the ticket, but it is often the largest long-term cost.

The ticket itself or the court's notice should list the amount due. If the paperwork shows only a fine and no costs, ask the clerk for the full payable total before you send money.

We do not list exact court-cost amounts for individual counties or cities. Those figures change by jurisdiction, and we only publish numbers we can source.

How many points does a speeding ticket add in Alabama?

Alabama uses a point system for moving violations. A speeding conviction adds 2 points if you were 25 or less over the limit, and 5 points if you were more than 25 over.

Points accumulate. Reaching 12–14 points in 2 years results in a 60-day suspension.

The 5-point tier matters most. One conviction in that tier puts you well along the way to the suspension threshold, and a single additional violation could push you into it.

  • 25 or less over: 2 points
  • More than 25 over: 5 points
  • 12–14 points within 2 years: 60-day suspension

For your current point total and the exact rules, check your driving record with the Alabama Law Enforcement Agency (ALEA). We do not track individual records.

Does a speeding ticket raise insurance in Alabama?

It can. A speeding conviction typically raises US auto premiums by roughly 20–25% on average at renewal, according to published market analyses.

That is a national average, not an Alabama figure. Your own change could be smaller or larger depending on your insurer, your record, and how far over the limit you were.

Insurers generally look at convictions, not just citations. How your case ends can therefore affect what you pay later, even though the court cost is a one-time amount.

We do not recommend specific insurers or predict your premium. For a quote that reflects a ticket, ask your own insurer or compare several.

Can an Alabama speeding ticket be dismissed or reduced?

Sometimes the court can resolve a ticket in a way that changes the fine, the points, or both. Whether that applies to you depends on the court, the facts, and your record.

Because the local court handling the citation sets the fine within statutory limits, its clerk is the best starting point. Ask what options exist, what the deadlines are, and what the total cost would be for each choice.

Questions about a defense or a plea are legal questions. TicketCostGuide is not a law firm and does not give legal advice or recommend attorneys. If you want advice on your case, consider speaking with a licensed Alabama attorney of your own choosing.

Whatever you decide, compare the likely outcomes. A lower fine with court costs on top may still cost more than expected, and a conviction can carry 2 or 5 points and affect insurance.

What happens if I don't pay an Alabama speeding ticket?

Ignoring a ticket usually makes the situation worse. Courts can add consequences when a citation goes unresolved, and an unresolved case can lead to problems with your license.

Exact deadlines, added penalties, and procedures differ by court, and we have no sourced figures for them. Check the citation and contact the clerk of the court named on it.

  • Read the ticket for the court name, the due date, and the ways to pay or respond.
  • Call the clerk if you are unsure of the amount or the deadline.
  • Ask about the full total, including court costs, before you pay.

Paying a ticket is generally treated as a conviction. That brings the points described above, so decide how to respond before the due date rather than after it.
