How much is a speeding ticket in Michigan?

A Michigan speeding ticket is a civil infraction, not a crime. The fines and costs are set by the district court that handles your citation, and totals are commonly in the $115–$250 range by mph over and court (Michigan Secretary of State).

That range is the realistic window for most drivers, but it is not a fixed schedule. Where you land inside it depends mainly on two things: how far over the limit you were and which court is handling the ticket.

The amount printed on your citation or on the court's payment notice is the number that applies to you. Treat any range, including the one on this page, as a guide only.

What drives the total on a Michigan speeding ticket?

Two factors do most of the work. The first is mph over the limit, which affects both the fine level and the points. The second is the court, because district court costs vary by court and make up much of the total.

This is why two drivers cited for the same speed can see different totals. It is also why we give a range rather than a single number for the whole state.

  • Mph over the limit: a larger overage generally means a higher total and more points.
  • The court: district court costs vary by court.
  • Your record: points from this ticket add to any already on your driving record.

The governing provisions are in MCL §257.627 / §257.320a, which cover speed limits and the point system.

Are there extra surcharges or driver responsibility fees?

Michigan repealed its separate driver responsibility fees. That means the court total is the whole bill, with no additional state assessment stacked on top for a speeding conviction.

Court costs are still part of that total, and they vary by court. They make up much of what you pay, so they are worth looking at on your citation or payment notice.

If you see a line item that you do not recognize, ask the court clerk what it covers. Clerks can explain the breakdown of a total, though they cannot give legal advice.

How many points does a speeding ticket add in Michigan?

Under MCL §257.320a, a speeding conviction adds 2–4 points by mph over. A lower overage falls toward the bottom of that range, and a higher overage falls toward the top.

Points matter because they accumulate. Reaching 12 points in 2 years leads to driver reexamination.

You can check your own record and status through the Michigan Secretary of State, which maintains driver records.

A single speeding ticket is far from the 12-point threshold, but points from several violations within 2 years can add up.

Does a speeding ticket raise insurance in Michigan?

It can. Published market analyses indicate that a speeding conviction typically raises US auto premiums by roughly 20–25% on average at renewal.

That is a national average, not a Michigan figure. Your own change could be smaller, larger, or nonexistent, depending on your insurer, your driving history and how the violation is treated on your record.

TicketCostGuide does not recommend insurers or predict what your carrier will do. If you are concerned, your insurer or agent can explain how it treats a violation at renewal.

Can a Michigan speeding ticket be dismissed or reduced?

Because it is a civil infraction handled by a district court, the court is where any dispute is decided. Your citation lists your response options and how to take them.

In general, drivers can pay the ticket, which counts as a conviction, or contest it with the court. Contesting does not guarantee a lower total or a dismissal.

  • Read the citation first: it explains your options and where to respond.
  • Contact the court: the clerk can explain procedures and the amount due.
  • Consider the points: the 2–4 points and possible insurance effect may matter as much as the fine.

For advice on your specific situation, speak with a licensed attorney. We do not recommend any specific attorney.

What happens if I don't pay a Michigan speeding ticket?

Ignoring a citation does not make it go away. An unresolved ticket can lead to further action by the court, which may mean added consequences beyond the original total.

The specifics depend on the court and the citation, so we do not list exact penalties here. The paperwork you received and the court clerk are the reliable places to confirm what applies.

If paying the full amount at once is a problem, ask the court what options it offers. Policies on this vary by court.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a speeding ticket in Michigan?

A Michigan speeding ticket is a civil infraction with fines and costs set by the district court. Totals are commonly in the $115–$250 range, depending on mph over and the court. Your citation or the court's notice shows the exact amount you owe.

How many points is a speeding ticket in Michigan?

Under MCL §257.320a, a speeding conviction adds 2–4 points, depending on how many mph over the limit you were. The higher the overage, the more points you can expect. Points from separate violations add together on your driving record.

How many points will suspend my license in Michigan?

Under the locked facts here, 12 points in 2 years leads to driver reexamination. A single speeding ticket adds 2–4 points, so reaching that level generally takes multiple violations. Check your record with the Michigan Secretary of State for your own point total.

Does Michigan still charge driver responsibility fees?

No. Michigan repealed its separate driver responsibility fees, so the court total is the whole bill. District court costs still apply and vary by court, and they make up much of what you pay on a speeding ticket.

Why do Michigan speeding ticket totals differ by court?

District court costs vary by court and make up much of the total. Mph over the limit also affects the fine. That is why totals are commonly in the $115–$250 range rather than a single fixed number statewide.

Will a speeding ticket raise my insurance in Michigan?

It may. Published market analyses suggest a speeding conviction typically raises US auto premiums by roughly 20–25% on average at renewal. That is a national average, not a Michigan figure, and your own result depends on your insurer and record.
