How much is a speeding ticket in Rhode Island?

Speeding fines in Rhode Island are set by a schedule used at the Traffic Tribunal. The schedule amounts are commonly about $85–$300+ by mph over, with minimum fines rising at 11+ over and technology/court fees added (Rhode Island Division of Motor Vehicles).

That range is the lead number for this page. It is a range and not a single price because the fine depends on how far over the limit you were driving and on what is added to the scheduled amount.

The fine itself is only one part of the cost. Court fees come on top, and a conviction can also affect your insurance bill later.

Your citation and any Tribunal notice show the amount you actually owe. Treat the range above as a guide, not a quote.

What drives the total cost of a Rhode Island speeding ticket?

Several things move the final number. The biggest is how many miles per hour over the limit you were cited for.

  • Speed over the limit. Higher mph over places you toward the top of the commonly cited range, and minimum fines rise at 11+ over.
  • Added fees. Court technology and assessment fees are added to the scheduled fine.
  • Your record. The Traffic Tribunal escalates sanctions for repeat offenses, including suspension.
  • Outcome of your case. A first-offense dismissal program is available at the Tribunal for clean records.

The state's statutes cover these rules. See R.I. Gen. Laws §31-14-2 and §31-41.1-4, available through Justia's Rhode Island code.

Because the total depends on your own citation, two drivers with similar tickets can end up paying different amounts.

What fees get added to the speeding fine?

Rhode Island adds court technology and assessment fees to the scheduled fine. These are separate from the fine itself, so the amount you pay is higher than the schedule figure alone.

The sources used for this guide do not give a single fixed amount for these fees. The amount varies by county/court and by citation, so we do not list a dollar figure for them.

To find the exact total, read the amounts printed on your citation or Tribunal paperwork. You can also contact the Traffic Tribunal clerk's office.

When you compare the cost of paying against the cost of contesting, include the added fees. They apply on top of the scheduled amount.

How many points does a speeding ticket add in Rhode Island?

Rhode Island has no running point system for ordinary speeding. That means a typical speeding ticket does not add a set number of points to your license the way it does in many other states.

The consequences come from the Traffic Tribunal instead. It escalates sanctions, including suspension, for repeat offenses.

So the absence of points does not mean repeat tickets are harmless. A clean record and a record with prior violations can lead to very different outcomes at the Tribunal.

If you want to compare how states handle speeding penalties, the Governors Highway Safety Association's state laws page is a useful starting point. For your own license status, check with the Rhode Island Division of Motor Vehicles.

Does a speeding ticket raise insurance rates in Rhode Island?

It can. Published market analyses indicate that a speeding conviction typically raises US auto premiums by roughly 20–25% on average at renewal.

That is a national average. It is not a Rhode Island figure, and we are not giving a state-specific estimate.

Your own change depends on your insurer, your driving history and how your policy treats violations. Some drivers see little change and some see more.

This is one reason the outcome of your case matters. A dismissal can mean no conviction on your record, while a conviction may show up when your policy renews. TicketCostGuide does not recommend any insurer, so ask your own provider how it treats violations.

Can a Rhode Island speeding ticket be dismissed or reduced?

First-offense dismissal programs are available at the Traffic Tribunal for clean records. If you have no prior violations, ask the Tribunal whether you qualify.

Eligibility rules and any conditions are set by the Tribunal, and we do not list them here. Your citation and the Tribunal's instructions explain how to request a hearing or apply.

A few practical steps can help:

  1. Read your citation closely for the alleged speed and the hearing information.
  2. Check whether your record is clean, since that is what the dismissal program is tied to.
  3. Ask the Tribunal clerk about options before you pay, because paying may be treated as admitting the violation.

This page is not legal advice. If you want advice on your specific case, a licensed attorney can give it.

What happens if I don't pay a Rhode Island speeding ticket?

Ignoring a ticket usually makes things worse. The Traffic Tribunal escalates sanctions for repeat offenses, including suspension, and an unresolved ticket can complicate your standing with the court.

The sources we used do not set out the exact penalties for non-payment, so we do not list specific consequences or deadlines. Your citation shows the dates and instructions that apply to you.

If you cannot pay in full, or you disagree with the ticket, contact the Traffic Tribunal before any deadline passes. Responding on time keeps more of your options open, including the first-offense dismissal program for clean records.

For license questions, such as whether a suspension is in effect, check with the Rhode Island Division of Motor Vehicles directly.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a speeding ticket in Rhode Island?

Traffic Tribunal schedule amounts are commonly about $85–$300+ by mph over, with minimum fines rising at 11+ over and technology/court fees added. Your exact total depends on your citation, so check the amounts printed on it or ask the Tribunal.

Does Rhode Island use points for speeding tickets?

No. Rhode Island has no running point system for ordinary speeding. Instead, the Traffic Tribunal escalates sanctions, including suspension, for repeat offenses. A single ticket does not add a set number of points, but repeat violations can bring tougher consequences.

What fees are added to a Rhode Island speeding fine?

Court technology and assessment fees are added to the scheduled fine. The amounts vary by county/court and by citation, so this guide does not list an exact dollar figure. Your citation or the Traffic Tribunal clerk can tell you the total.

Will a speeding ticket raise my insurance in Rhode Island?

It may. Published market analyses show a speeding conviction typically raises US auto premiums by roughly 20–25% on average at renewal. That is a national average, not a Rhode Island figure. Your actual change depends on your insurer and your record.

Can I get a first speeding ticket dismissed in Rhode Island?

First-offense dismissal programs are available at the Traffic Tribunal for clean records. Eligibility is set by the Tribunal, so ask the clerk or read your citation's instructions before paying. This is general information, not legal advice.

Can repeat speeding tickets lead to a suspension in Rhode Island?

Yes, it is possible. The Traffic Tribunal escalates sanctions, including suspension, for repeat offenses, even though Rhode Island has no running point system for ordinary speeding. Check with the Division of Motor Vehicles about your own license status.

Where can I find the Rhode Island speeding law?

The relevant provisions are R.I. Gen. Laws §31-14-2 and §31-41.1-4, which you can read through Justia's Rhode Island code. The Rhode Island Division of Motor Vehicles also publishes information on the Traffic Tribunal's speeding fine schedule.
