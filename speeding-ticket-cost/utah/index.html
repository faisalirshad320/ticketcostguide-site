How much is a speeding ticket in Utah?

Utah's Uniform Fine Schedule sets speeding fines at roughly $90–$470, depending on how many miles per hour over the limit you were driving (Utah Driver License Division). The schedule is tied to Utah Code §41-6a-601 and the Uniform Fine Schedule.

The low end of the range is for driving only a little over the limit. The high end is for much faster speeds, or for tickets where an enhancement applies.

That range is the useful number to plan around. It is not a quote for your ticket, because your exact amount depends on your recorded speed, where you were stopped, and how the court handles the citation.

Check the citation itself and your court's payment instructions for the exact amount. This guide does not list a fixed local figure for any city or county.

What drives the total on a Utah speeding ticket?

Three things move the number most: how far over the limit you were, how fast you were going, and where you were driving.

  • Mph over the limit. The schedule steps up as the gap between your speed and the posted limit grows.
  • Higher-speed enhancements. The schedule includes enhancements at 21+ and 30+ mph over.
  • Work and school zones. Tickets in these zones carry enhancements as well.

These enhancements are part of the schedule, so you should not expect a separate add-on for each one. The same is true of surcharges and fees: they are built into the schedule amounts, not stacked on top as separate items in the figures we use.

Your own ticket is the best check. If the amount on it looks different from what you expected, ask the court clerk how it was calculated.

Are there extra surcharges and fees?

On the Uniform Fine Schedule, zone enhancements and fees are built into the listed amounts. In practice, the amount you see on a schedule line already accounts for them.

We do not publish a separate dollar figure for surcharges, because the sources we rely on do not break one out. Anything beyond the schedule amount, such as a payment-method fee or a cost tied to a court option you choose, would be set by the court you deal with.

If you are weighing options, ask the court exactly what the total is and what it includes before you pay. Get the answer in writing or on the payment page when you can.

For a wider view of how states treat traffic offenses, the Governors Highway Safety Association state laws page is a useful reference.

How many points does a speeding ticket add in Utah?

Speeding carries 35–75 points on Utah's scale, depending on how many mph over the limit you were (Utah Driver License Division). Faster speeds sit toward the higher end.

Utah's point scale uses big numbers compared with many states, but the idea is the same: a handful of tickets can put your license at risk.

The key threshold is 200 points in 3 years, which leads to a suspension for drivers age 21 and older. Points from several tickets add together within that window.

Rough math: because a single speeding ticket falls in the 35–75 point range, a few tickets close together can approach the suspension threshold. Check your own record with the Driver License Division for your actual total.

This page does not cover rules for drivers under 21 or for commercial license holders. Look those up with the Driver License Division directly.

Does a speeding ticket raise insurance in Utah?

It often does. Published market analyses show that a speeding conviction typically raises US auto premiums by roughly 20–25% on average at renewal.

That is a national average, not a Utah-specific number. Your own change could be higher, lower, or nothing, depending on your insurer, your driving history, and how the ticket is resolved.

The increase usually shows up when your policy renews, not the day you pay the fine. So the true cost of a ticket can be much larger than the fine alone.

TicketCostGuide does not recommend any insurer. If you are concerned, ask your own insurer how a ticket would be treated, and compare it with the fine amount when you decide how to respond.

Can a Utah speeding ticket be dismissed or reduced?

Sometimes, but the outcome depends on the facts and on the court. We do not give legal advice and cannot say whether your ticket qualifies for anything.

The citation tells you how to respond and where. Read it closely, because the court named on it handles your case.

  • Ask the court clerk what response options exist for your type of citation.
  • Ask how any option would affect the amount you owe and the points on your record.
  • If you want advice on your specific situation, speak with a licensed attorney of your own choosing.

Because points and insurance can matter as much as the fine, it is worth comparing the full picture. A lower fine that still leaves points on your record may not be the cheapest result overall.

What happens if I don't pay a Utah speeding ticket?

Ignoring a ticket generally makes things worse. Courts can add consequences when a citation goes unanswered, and your license status can be affected.

We do not list specific deadlines or penalty amounts here, because the sources we rely on do not give a single figure that applies everywhere. Your citation and the court named on it set the dates that apply to you.

If you cannot pay the full amount, contact the court before the due date and ask what is available. Courts are usually easier to work with when you reach out early.

Remember also that a ticket adds points toward the 200 points in 3 years suspension threshold for drivers age 21 and older, so an unresolved ticket is not only a money problem.
