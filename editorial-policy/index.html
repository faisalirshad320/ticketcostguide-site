Editorial Policy

Editorial Policy

By Editorial Team, TicketCostGuide · Last reviewed 2026-10-18

Every dollar figure on TicketCostGuide is sourced from official material: state statutes and vehicle codes, state DMV/DPS publications, state court system fee schedules, and county court fee schedules. Each page lists its sources with links and shows a "last reviewed" date.

Our sourcing rules

  • Statute first. Base fine ranges come from the state's vehicle code or the court system's uniform fine schedule.
  • Surcharges itemized. Mandatory add-ons (court costs, assessments, state surcharges) are listed separately, because they often exceed the base fine.
  • "Varies by county" when it does. Where courts set amounts locally, we state the statutory range and say so — we never invent a single exact figure.
  • Dated reviews. Pages carry the month and year the figures were last verified against their sources.

Who writes the content

Pages are compiled and maintained by the TicketCostGuide editorial team from the official sources above. We are not a law firm and do not provide legal advice.

Corrections

Report errors via our contact page. Verified corrections are published promptly and the review date updated.

This page is general information, not legal advice. Fines, surcharges and court costs vary by county and court — always verify the amount on your citation or with the court listed on it. See our full disclaimer and editorial policy.
