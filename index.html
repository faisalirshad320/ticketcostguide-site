How Much Does a Traffic Ticket Cost?

By Editorial Team, TicketCostGuide · Last reviewed 2026-10-15

A traffic ticket in the US typically costs $50 to $500 for the base fine, but surcharges, court costs and state fees often double the total — and some violations (reckless driving, no insurance, high-speed offenses) run $500–$2,500+. What this site covers

TicketCostGuide documents the real, current cost of traffic tickets and court fines in all 50 states: base fines set by statute, the mandatory surcharges courts add, license points, how long tickets stay on your record, typical insurance increases, how to pay, and legal ways to get tickets reduced or dismissed. Every figure cites the state statute, DMV publication or court fee schedule it came from, with a last-reviewed date.
